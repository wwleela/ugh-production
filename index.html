Urban Gliding Hyderabad (UGH) is Hyderabad's doorstep action sports coaching initiative — bringing skateboarding, inline skating, and BMX training directly to residential communities, schools, and skate parks across the city.

Through partnerships with local skate parks, UGH runs structured workshops and classes for all ages. UGH is now expanding into schools with curriculum-based action sports programs, and into residential apartments with doorstep classes — so kids get hands-on coaching, and parents get their time back, all without leaving the community.

Skateboarding Classes in Hyderabad

Skateboarding in India is growing faster than ever. Grassroots collectives like Holystoked Collective and park-builders like 100 Ramp have driven the construction of dozens of DIY and professional concrete skate parks across Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, and Goa. The sport's entry onto the Olympic stage has accelerated a shift from casual street riding toward organized competitive training and national representation — and UGH's Hyderabad coaching programs sit directly in that pipeline.

BMX Coaching in Hyderabad

Action sports in India are in the middle of a quiet revolution, and BMX is leading it — moving from a niche street subculture into an organized, mainstream competitive sport.

Hyderabad is already part of that story with teen BMX racer Agasthi Chandrasekhar securing top finishes at the USA Grand Nationals, Arizona Nationals, and Asian-level events in Malaysia.

UGH's BMX program covers pump track rhythm, bunny hops, and manual control for riders at every stage.

Frequently Asked Questions about Skating Classes

What gear is mandatory for classes? expand_more
A certified helmet, wrist guards, elbow pads, and knee pads are required for every class — no exceptions.
What's the age limit? expand_more
UGH coaches Junior (5–12 yrs), Senior (13–17 yrs), and Adult riders/skaters. Everyone is welcome to learn.
What happens if it rains? expand_more
Rained-out sessions are rescheduled to a make-up day so you don't lose your training time.
What should I bring to my first session? expand_more
Comfortable athletic clothing and closed-toe shoes. Protective gear is provided, or you can bring your own per our mandatory safety policy.
Does UGH offer workshops at Aparna Serene Park? expand_more
Yes — a weekly skateboarding workshop has actually been run at Aparna Serene Park, Kondapur.
Does UGH offer workshops at Gachibowli schools? expand_more
Yes, as part of our Noble Practices initiative — a real outreach program that has taught 60+ students free skateboarding instruction.
Does UGH offer workshops at Aparna Heights 1? expand_more
We are currently booking workshops for Aparna Heights 1, Kondapur — available on request.
