FAQs

What gear is mandatory? +

A certified helmet, wrist guards, elbow pads, and knee pads are non-negotiable for every class. No exceptions.

What is the age limit? +

We teach Junior (5-12 yrs), Senior (13-17 yrs), and Adult gliders. Everyone is welcome to learn.

What happens if it rains? +

In case of rain, sessions will be rescheduled to a make-up day, ensuring you don't miss out on your training.

What should I bring to my first session? +

Please wear comfortable athletic clothing and closed-toe shoes. All necessary protective gear is provided, or you must bring your own per our mandatory safety policy.

No Gear, No Skate

Safety & Protection

We strictly enforce a mandatory 4-point CE/EN certified protection policy. All skaters must use non-marking PU wheels (82A-86A) to protect court surfaces.

Helmet

A certified, well-fitted helmet is required to protect against impacts.

Full Pad Kit

Wrist guards, elbow pads, and knee pads are essential to prevent scrapes and fractures.
